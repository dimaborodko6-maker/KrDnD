The [Wizards Fan Content Policy](https://company.wizards.com/en/legal/fancontentpolicy) contains restrictions on incorporating its IP into other games and on trademarks; it should not be assumed to authorize this downloadable game simply because it is free. The [official SRD page](https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd) describes separately licensed Creative Commons rules content and attribution requirements. A content-by-content review is needed before choosing that route. Neither source grants rights to Kingdom Rush material.

The [Ironhide official site](https://www.ironhidegames.com/) identifies its games and contact route. No written Ironhide permission was supplied in the archive. Attribution alone is not evidence of permission. Renaming a page title or removing an unused image does not establish that the remaining product has been cleared.

Before an open download or sale, record the chosen project title, copyright holder, project license, any permissions, the precise SRD/other licensed material used, required credits, and a support/bug-report destination. Recheck these sources on the actual publication date. Do not attach a “permitted fan content” notice unless that permission basis has been verified for this project.

For a private test with friends, use the prepared local-play candidate and gather feedback. The public launch decision remains with the owner. This preparation did not contact rights holders, upload files, create a public repository or publish a release.
